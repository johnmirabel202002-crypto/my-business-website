4 Steps To Salon Perfection

1

Prep Nails

Push back cuticles, lightly buff nail surface, and sanitize with the included alcohol pad.

2

Size Your Set

Match each Nail'e press-on tip to your natural nail width for a seamless fit.

3

Apply Glue / Tab

4
