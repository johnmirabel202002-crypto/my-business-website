4 Steps to Salon Perfection

1. Prep Nails

Clean and gently push back your cuticles for a smooth base.

2. Select Size

Match the correct press-on size to each of your fingers.

3. Apply Glue / Tab

Apply a drop of nail glue or place sticky tab onto your natural nail bed.

4. Press & Slay

Align press-on at a 45-degree angle under cuticle and hold firmly for 20 seconds!
