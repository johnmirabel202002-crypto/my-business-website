Your at-home nail ritual

4 Steps to Salon Perfection

Take your time, follow the instructions included with your nail set, and enjoy your finished look.

01

Prep Your Nails

Start with clean, dry natural nails. Gently care for your cuticles and remove any oils from the nail surface.

02

Choose Your Sizes

Match each press-on nail to the correct finger before you begin applying the set.

03

Apply Carefully

Use the nail adhesive or sticky tabs according to the product instructions and avoid contact with skin.

04

Enjoy Your Set

Position each nail carefully and press as directed by the adhesive instructions. Keep your set safe for reuse.
